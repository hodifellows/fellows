# Code of Conduct

Hodi Fellows is a place to learn in public, which means making mistakes in public. We keep it safe to do that.

## We expect everyone to

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are newer than you.
- Review code, not people. Explain what to change and why.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give credit, including to the people who helped you.
- Respect privacy: share only your own information.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, mockery, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Unwanted contact, or sharing someone's personal details.
- Taking credit for someone else's work.

## Reporting

If something makes you uncomfortable, email **hello@hodifellows.com**. Reports are handled privately. Anyone who breaks this code may be asked to leave the programme.
